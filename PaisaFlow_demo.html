How AiNow picks an account

When you schedule a vendor payment, AiNow recommends the smallest account that can still fully cover the payment — this keeps your larger reserve accounts free for bigger upcoming obligations instead of draining them for small bills. If no single account has enough balance, it flags the shortfall so you can top up or split the payment manually.
