The problem

Collections run on memory, not on a system.

Pakistani SMEs extend credit to keep clients, then chase that same credit manually, by phone, by WhatsApp, by whoever remembers to follow up. There is no system that predicts who is about to pay late, or drafts the reminder in the language the client actually responds to.

03
The moat

The hard part is the part you cannot see.

💬

Speaks the client's language, not just yours

A reminder in English gets ignored by a client who thinks in Roman Urdu. PaisaFlow drafts in the language and tone that actually gets a reply, not a translation afterthought.

🔍

Explains the risk, does not just flag it

Every risk score comes with the weighted reasons behind it, historical delay, sector volatility, concentration, so a finance team sees exactly why a client was flagged, not just that it was.

🔒

A human approves every send

The AI drafts and suggests. It never sends a reminder or schedules a payment without a person confirming the tone, channel and account first.

Phase 1

  • Draft reminders without sending
Phase 2

Extend

  • Enable AI-drafted sends
  • Connect vendor bills and bank balances
  • Turn on payment-account suggestions
Phase 3

Standardise

PaisaFlow becomes the system every invoice and every bill passes through.

  • Every client scored automatically
  • Every reminder logged by default
